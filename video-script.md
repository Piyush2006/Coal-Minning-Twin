# Video Script — "Living Digital Twins for Industrial Operations"

**Product:** Faclon Digital Twin Creator (IOsense)
**Runtime:** ~2:10 · ~290 words · ~135 wpm (calm, confident corporate VO)
**Reference structure:** NVIDIA "Industrial Digital Twins for Simulating Robot Fleets"
**Footage source:** Blackridge Coal Mine twin (pit → CHPP → stockyard → rail → port)

---

## [0:01 – 0:15] — The opening claim

**VO:**
> Faclon Labs, the industrial IoT solutions company, is bringing living,
> data-connected digital twins to the world's most demanding industrial
> operations — from mines and processing plants to ports.

**Visuals:** Slow aerial pull-back over the full Blackridge twin — benched pit
in the foreground, CHPP mid-frame, rail and shiploader on the horizon. Faclon +
IOsense logos fade in over the skyline.

---

## [0:16 – 0:41] — The problem

**VO:**
> Managing a high-performance mining operation means navigating a complex web
> of decisions, influenced by constantly shifting variables. These include
> fluctuating ore grades and product quality, equipment health and
> availability, energy and water consumption, and the coordination of fixed
> plant, mobile fleets, and outbound logistics. And seeing the true, live state
> of an operation that stretches from pit to port is nearly impossible today.

**Visuals:** Fast cuts matching each variable — excavator dig cycle in the pit;
CHPP module with steam plumes; thickener rakes turning; haul trucks following
their paths; the train rake under the load-out. End on a cluttered
control-room/SCADA screen for "nearly impossible."

---

## [0:42 – 0:52] — The solution

**VO:**
> To tackle these challenges, operators are adopting the Digital Twin Creator —
> a Faclon IOsense application for building live industrial digital twins,
> generated by AI and connected to real plant data.

**Visuals:** UI reveal — the Creator home screen, then the twin loading in:
terrain first, then plant, then fleets snapping into place.

---

## [0:53 – 1:07] — How it works, step 1: AI generation

**VO:**
> First, operators simply describe their operation. The Creator's AI pipeline
> plans the site, authors every component — from excavators and haul trucks to
> crushers, conveyors, and shiploaders — and generates a complete, animated
> 3D twin in minutes, not months.

**Visuals:** A one-line prompt being typed; the PLAN → COMPONENT → GENERATE
stages ticking through; component thumbnails populating; the finished scene
assembling itself.

---

## [1:08 – 1:20] — Step 2: structured like the plant

**VO:**
> The twin is organized the way the operation is — a hierarchy of sites, areas,
> cells, and assets, with every machine publishing its signals to a unified
> namespace.

**Visuals:** The scene tree expanding — Site → Area → Cell → Asset — while the
camera dives from full-site view down to a single excavator. A UNS topic path
types itself out on screen.

---

## [1:21 – 1:44] — Step 3: the live loop

**VO:**
> Live telemetry streams from sensors and PLCs into the twin. Machines move as
> the plant moves. Rules evaluate every signal — a hot crusher bearing, a truck
> running low on fuel, rising product ash — raising alerts on the exact
> machine, the moment conditions change.

**Visuals:** Telemetry tooltips over hero machines updating in real time;
conveyor-vision CCTV feed with detection highlights; then HT-03's amber
severity ring firing and the alerts panel sliding in.

---

## [1:45 – 2:00] — The payoff

**VO:**
> Now operators can see the entire operation at a glance — measuring live KPIs
> such as plant feed, yield, throughput, and stock on ground — and act on
> issues before they ever reach production.

**Visuals:** Camera glides pit-to-port in one continuous move with KPI
overlays per area; every state ring green except the one resolving amber.

---

## [2:01 – 2:10] — The close

**VO:**
> With IOsense and the Digital Twin Creator, Faclon Labs is reinventing how
> industry sees itself.

**Visuals:** Final wide of the whole living twin at golden hour, machines still
moving. Logo lockup + tagline. Music swell, cut to black.

---

## Swap-in points

- **Customer name:** written operator-generic; if a launch customer signs off,
  swap "operators are adopting" → "**[Customer]**, the **[descriptor]**, is
  adopting" to fully match the NVIDIA/KION framing (their opening names the
  customer first).
- **Market stat:** the NVIDIA script opens with "$1 trillion warehouse market."
  A verified equivalent (e.g., global mining output value) can be inserted in
  the opening line — verify the figure with marketing before use.
- **Industry:** "mining operation" generalizes to any vertical the twin ships
  for; the problem-variables list is the only industry-specific sentence.
